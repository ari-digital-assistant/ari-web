# The wake word

Say "Hey Ari" — the default wake word — and Ari wakes up, hands-free. "Ok
Ari" is also available if you'd rather use that instead, and "Hey Jarvis"
ships as a fallback model.

Wake word detection happens **on the device**. Your phone is listening for
that one phrase, locally — there's no always-on microphone feed going to the
cloud, and nothing is sent anywhere until Ari has actually been woken up.

## Hands-free and the lock screen

Ari can wake from the lock screen, so you don't need to unlock your phone
first — just say the wake word and start talking.

## "Let's talk" mode

Once you've started a conversation, "Let's talk" mode keeps it going: you
can ask follow-up questions without repeating the wake word every time. If
Ari is speaking and you want to jump in, barge-in lets you interrupt and say
your next thing — no need to wait for it to finish.

## Tips

- A quieter room makes wake word detection more reliable, though it's built
  to cope with everyday background noise.
- Not keen on the default? You can change the wake word at any time in
  Settings.
